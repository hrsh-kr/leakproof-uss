# Q2, Advocate B1 (A2 Graham + P1 PM): 3 roles, ruthless

## (a) Strongest case
- **Marks go to a flow a cold tester can drive.** Usability (25%) is judged on the guide; security (25%) by attackers who try login, authz, XSS and devtools first (V2). Three polished roles with a route test per endpoint beat five roles with one cracked page.
- **Each role costs a seed account, a matrix-test column, a guide chapter and screens.** U1's five-role plan is 9 screens plus timeline, drawer and sandbox provisioning. P1 has about 4 build days, one builder, freeze 15 Nov.
- **Test week needs a working reset, not a grand story.** A dead link or broken reset loses usability marks before any crypto does (V2 (b)).

## (b) Best attacks on B2
1. **Per-tester sandboxes with every role pre-created put all five logins in one tester's hands.** That erases the separation of duties the security marks reward (V2 concedes "defeats separation"). It also adds provisioning (8+ testers x 5 accounts x reset) and a new bug class, cross-sandbox isolation, which classmates will hunt. A shared seeded exam plus a reset button gives the same walkthrough far cheaper.
2. **The headline is a function, but the proposal builds a role around it.** `traceSeats`/`visibleFingerprint` already exist in `logic.mjs`. A3 names partial-photo matching as the top risk, yet U1 adds ranked results, confirm/dismiss, notes and thresholds. The auditor persona is the expensive part and is not what earns the video moment.

## (c) Concession
B2 is right that tracing is the most memorable demo, that setter confidentiality (V2 S3: setter B gets 403 on A's question) needs a real setter endpoint, and that one tester cannot honestly form a quorum without playing several roles. The guide must say that openly.

## (d) Compromise I could live with
**3 full roles, 2 thin roles on the same auth, one shared seeded exam, trace as an action.**
- Full UI and route tests: admin, custodian, superintendent.
- Thin setter: one form, submit and list own items, enforced server-side. ~0.5 day.
- Thin auditor: one read-only page, "verify chain" plus "trace from pasted text" (ranked seats, "a lead, not a verdict"; no confirm/notes). ~0.75 day with 3-question-fragment tests.
- "Reset demo" button, plus a labelled "demo mode: act as all custodians" switch; separate logins still work for multi-person runs. ~0.5 day.
- No per-tester sandboxes, no timeline strip (one stage banner), no pipeline drawer.

About 1.75 days over the 3-role plan, taken from the 15 Nov security slot, not the freeze. Cut order if 14 Nov slips: auditor UI first (trace becomes a recorded CLI demo), then setter page (seeded questions).

## (e) Biggest risk
The thin roles become the weakest pages (auditor endpoint leaking question text, setter list showing others' items), and attackers go there first. Mitigation: they join the matrix test on day one, and an untested thin role is deleted, not shipped.
