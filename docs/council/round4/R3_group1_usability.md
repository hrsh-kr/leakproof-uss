# R3: FCS Group 1 (Meridian / Secure Job Portal), usability and usable-security catalogue

Lens: H1/U1, HCI usable security and UI/UX. Role-played lens for a student project, not the view of a real person. Research only; no FCS script was run.

**Path shorthands.** `G1/` = `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-hrshkr-Downloads-sem8-uss/29dd3dd2-a7a9-4bdd-b465-6e19cbfa674a/scratchpad/g1/Secure-Job-Portal-main/`, `fe/` = `G1/frontend/src/`, `be/` = `G1/backend/app/`. **Ours:** `app.js`, `styles.css` and `index.html` are in `/Users/hrshkr/Downloads/sem8/uss/project/app/public/`, server files are in `/Users/hrshkr/Downloads/sem8/uss/project/app/src/`. **CSV:** `L<n>` = line n of `/Users/hrshkr/Downloads/sem8/uss/project/fcs/fcs_bugs.csv` (Group 1 rows only; reporter names are left out).

**What I read.** All 30 files under `fe/`. Every `HTTPException(detail=…)` in `be/`, plus `be/security/rate_limiter.py`, `be/security/password.py` and `be/routers/auth.py` (lockout). `G1/README.md`. A grep of `G1/docs/report/sections/*.tex` for usability content, which turned up almost none: the report treats UX as a by-product. All 72 Group 1 rows of the CSV.

**One-line verdict on Group 1.** Their UI looks polished (glass cards, toasts, a custom confirm dialog, a randomised keypad). The usable-security work is thin, and in one place it is false: a "End-to-end encrypted" badge sits on chat messages that are sent as plaintext. Classmates filed 31 Functionality reports against them. Most were really about **orientation and system status**: testers could not tell whether the site was down, rate-limited, or simply lacked the role they needed. LeakProof already beats Group 1 on most of the patterns that matter. The useful lessons are in the failures.

## 2. Usability failures classmates reported against Group 1, with the lesson

All Group 1 Functionality rows (31 Invalid, 8 Valid), plus the Security rows that are really about usability. Rows are grouped by cause.

| Cluster | CSV rows (ruling) | What happened (evidence) | Lesson for LeakProof |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. "Site broken" during peer DoS** | L2, L3, L7, L20, L37, L41, L56 (all Invalid) | Under classmates' floods the site returned 5xx, 502 (L43) or nothing. The UI showed "Login failed" / "Registration failed" (`Login.jsx:30`, `Register.jsx:32`) with no hint that the server, not the user, was at fault. The team's rebuttal: "you were probably testing during a DoS" | **Visibility of system status.** A failed fetch or 502/503 must read as "the server is busy or unreachable", never as a user error. | **B. Rate limiting and lockout invisible** | L26 (Invalid; rebuttal: "rate limiting is present. | **E.
