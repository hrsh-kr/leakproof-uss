# P1 (PM), round 3: attack on the minimal system

**Is the saving real?** About 0.7 to 1 day, not 1 to 1.5. Gross cuts (second ceremony 0.4, centres and identity gate 0.3, commit-reveal 0.25, uploads 0.2, assignments screen 0.15) are real. Added back: 80 seeded testers with five card slots each, reset script, practice labelling (about 0.4), and capabilities instead of roles saves nothing (same table, same middleware). Worse, the saving lands on 13 and 14 Nov. Day 12 (six crypto fixes, login, CSRF, middleware, route-matrix test, TA email, token rotation) got no relief and is already half gone.

## 1. Biggest hole: practice mode contradicts the core claim
Section 2 says one account owns at most one share. Section 4 gives each tester account all five slots, while G1 needs "three different key holders". Either G1 counts slots (so SR4 "one account cannot supply two shares" is untestable in the environment classmates actually attack) or testers cannot release at all. Scenario: tester07 claims a card, refreshes, and the "once" card is gone. They report an unrecoverable lock, and the builder must reset it by hand while 80 testers and one builder are in the 18 to 25 Nov window. The same loss on 26 Nov kills the live demo.

## 2. Cut too far
- Card recovery. Make the card re-servable to its owner; store the encrypted blob. Less code, and no reset load.
- Any usability time. The Release page carries the 25% and is built on 14 Nov as an afterthought; the token/visual pass from doc 10 §6.6.1 vanished from the schedule.

## 3. Not cut far enough
- Browser-side card protocol (passphrase KDF, text format, two-word check): the riskiest new client crypto. Pre-decide its fallback now.
- G3 seat count: the person who prints also types the number. Theatre. Keep as a logged field, do not claim it as a security gate.
- `close` stage and route: allow trace after RELEASED.
- Trace UI: exact-fingerprint matching only.
- 80 accounts: the loop is cheap; the reset ops are not. Ship 40, regenerate on request.

## 4. Critical path and first slip
VM reply and HTTPS (crypto.subtle needs it) -> card protocol -> release gates -> copies -> trace -> final UI -> guide screenshots -> pilot -> post. First slip: **14 Nov**, which holds cards, request, approval, gates, copies, seed and deploy in one day. Spill eats the 15 Nov break-it day and the trace.

## 5. Minimum shippable (if it goes wrong)
VM plus HTTPS, login, capability middleware, submit with counter, Seal, 3-of-5 release with passphrase typed at approval (server unwraps), window gate, per-seat copies, public log, guide, known-issues list. Cut in order: trace UI (scripted `make trace`), browser card, G3, close, tester count.

2. Send the TA email and decide the TLS plan today (0.1).
