How to use this site

Three steps, then your feedback.

Use it

  1. Watch the animation. It plays by itself. Pause, or click a step, to read it.
  2. Try the four ideas. Tap and slide. Add your own question if you like.
  3. Tell us what you think: a 12 to 15 minute review (you get a participation receipt), or book a 20-minute chat.

How to give the best feedback

  • Say what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Point to the exact moment you got stuck or confused.
  • Be blunt. We are testing the design, not you.
  • Tell us what you would change first.
  • Short on time? Tap Not quite under any idea and leave a one-line note.
