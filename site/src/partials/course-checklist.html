Phase I checklist

Everything the brief asks for, in one place

Built from the course brief, the lectures, our Assignment 1 and outside practice for usability reports. Green means done. Amber means built and waiting on responses. Grey means not started.

  • ✓
    Project detailsThe problem, who it affects, what we are building and why. Source of truth: real cases.
  • ✓
    Workflow and protocol flowchartAnimated pipeline plus the step-by-step flow for each of five roles, with decisions and failure paths.
  • ✓
    Low-fidelity prototypeStoryboard and role flows. A printable paper pack for watched sessions is still to add.
  • ✓
    Mid-fidelity clickable prototypeScreens for setter, custodian, centre and investigator, with design decisions and what to challenge.
  • ✓
    Working prototypeFour demos with real encryption, key splitting and a hash chain. Tested.
  • …
    Study designGoals, participants, consent, tasks with scenario, goal, end line and time guide, data collected. A pilot with two people is still to run.
  • …
    Primary research resultsCollected by the review. Live numbers fill in as people respond. Interviews and watched sessions are planned.
  • ✓
    Secondary researchIncident analysis and comparative analysis with sources. Literature list in progress.
  • …
    Critical incidents and mental modelsCollected in the review (what they tried, expected and saw; where they think leaks happen). Consolidated with severity after responses are coded.
  • …
    Insights and design themesFirst SWOT drawn. The "you said, we changed" list fills in as feedback arrives.
  • ✓
    RequirementsUsability, security, privacy and functional, each with a test or evaluation method. Evidence IDs are added as findings are coded.
  • ✓
    Threat model, assumptions, limits24 threats with controls, assumptions that say what breaks if false, and openly stated limits.
  • ✓
    Decision logSeventeen decisions with options, pros and cons, and what would change our mind.
  • ✓
    LimitationsProxy participants, small self-selected sample, simulated data, a remote test that cannot observe.
  • ✓
    Participation evidence for classmatesEach participant gets a receipt with study, team, date, time and code. How credit is logged is a question for the TAs.
For the reviewer

A five-minute tour

  1. Open How it works and let the animation play. Pause on step 6 to read the release conditions.
  2. Open Prototypes, choose Centre, and try to print with a custodian missing.
  3. Open Research and read the cases and the comparison. Then check the live results.
  4. Open Requirements and expand any decision.
  5. Open Review to see how we ask for feedback, and take it if you like.

Questions we are asking the TAs: how participation in other groups' studies is verified for credit; whether Phase I needs a separate proposal or ethics application; whether a website plus PDF is acceptable; whether a team of four is fine.
