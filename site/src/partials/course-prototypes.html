How we chose the fidelity

Deeper where it is riskiest.

Following Lecture 3, we keep early screens simple (horizontal: every role has a screen, with little depth) and go deeper (vertical) on the riskiest flow, the centre print ceremony, because that is where a tired person, a clock and a refusal meet.

  • Low fidelity: the storyboard above and the role task flows on How it works.
  • Medium fidelity: the clickable screens above (simulated data, real logic for the print checks and the leak match).
  • Working prototypes: the four demos on Try it use real encryption, real key splitting and a real hash chain.

Everything here is tested with classmates in the review, and the results are on the Research page.
